How to Order Coffee in Korean

A café order is a small conversation with several choices: drink, quantity, temperature and whether you will stay. You can begin with one clear sentence and add details as needed. You do not need a complicated grammar pattern to make a polite request.

In this lesson

  1. Use a drink name plus 주세요
  2. Choose hot or iced
  3. Stay or take away
  4. Make changes without overcomplicating
  5. Rehearse the whole exchange
  6. Listen to examples
  7. Quick self-check

Use a drink name plus 주세요

아메리카노 한 잔 주세요 means “One Americano, please.” 주세요 is a useful polite request pattern. The counter 잔 is used for cups or glasses of drinks. One becomes 한 before this counter, so use 한 잔 rather than 하나 잔. On a noisy day, pointing at the menu while saying the order is perfectly practical.

Choose hot or iced

아이스 아메리카노 is a common iced Americano order. 따뜻한 아메리카노 asks for a hot one. Staff may ask 아이스로 드릴까요? to confirm an iced drink. Reply 네, 아이스로 주세요 if that is what you want. You may hear loanwords pronounced in Korean rhythm; do not expect the stress pattern of English “Americano.”

Stay or take away

포장해 주세요 asks for takeaway. 매장에서 마실게요 says that you will drink in the café. Staff may ask 드시고 가세요? to check whether you will stay. Listening for 포장 and 매장 helps you recognize the choice even before you understand every part of the question.

Make changes without overcomplicating

시럽 빼 주세요 means “Please leave out the syrup.” This is a request, not a guarantee that every item can be changed. If an ingredient matters for an allergy, explain it explicitly and confirm with the staff. For ordinary preferences, a short request is often easier to understand than a long memorized sentence.

Rehearse the whole exchange

Customer: 아이스 아메리카노 한 잔 주세요. Staff: 드시고 가세요? Customer: 아니요, 포장해 주세요. Finish with 감사합니다 after receiving the drink. Practise each turn separately, then say the customer turns from memory. Change only the drink or quantity at first so that you keep the useful sentence frame stable.

Listen to useful examples

  • 따뜻한 걸로 주세요.ttatteuthan geollo juseyo

A common mistake

Do not use 하나 잔 for one cup. Use 한 잔. Listen for the choice in a staff question instead of automatically answering 네 to every sentence you hear.

Quick self-check

Answer all three questions, then check your understanding. This is practice, not a proficiency certificate.

1. Which means one cup?

하나 changes to 한 before the counter 잔.

2. What asks for takeaway?

포장 asks to have the item packed to take away.

3. Which word helps you recognize an iced drink?

아이스 is the Korean loanword used for iced drinks.
