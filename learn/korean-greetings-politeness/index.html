Korean Greetings and Politeness: 안녕하세요

A useful greeting does more than translate “hello.” It helps you choose a comfortable level of politeness with the person in front of you. For a beginner talking to an unfamiliar adult, polite Korean is a practical starting point.

In this lesson

  1. Begin with 안녕하세요
  2. Choose polite language first
  3. Thank and apologize naturally
  4. Say goodbye according to who leaves
  5. Try a small exchange
  6. Listen to examples
  7. Quick self-check

Begin with 안녕하세요

안녕하세요 works for many everyday greetings, including meeting a shop worker, a neighbour or someone you have just been introduced to. A small nod can accompany it. It does not have to be a long or dramatic performance. Say the whole phrase smoothly, rather than separating every written syllable.

Choose polite language first

해요체, the polite style commonly ending in 요, is used widely in everyday conversation. 합니다-style speech is more formal and appears in announcements, presentations and formal situations. Casual speech, or 반말, fits certain close relationships. Being younger than someone is not a reason to begin casually with them. Wait until the relationship clearly supports it.

Thank and apologize naturally

감사합니다 is a widely useful polite thank-you. 고마워요 is another polite choice. 죄송해요 or 죄송합니다 can express an apology. 실례합니다 is useful for politely interrupting or passing through a space. These expressions overlap in some situations but are not interchangeable in every context. Think about what you are doing, not only the English word.

Say goodbye according to who leaves

안녕히 가세요 is said to someone who is leaving. 안녕히 계세요 is said to someone who is staying when you leave. If both people are leaving, 안녕히 가세요 is a common choice. A shop worker may use a different friendly closing, so do not expect every real conversation to follow one fixed script.

Try a small exchange

You enter a small shop: 안녕하세요. You receive your purchase: 감사합니다. You leave while the owner stays: 안녕히 계세요. Practise the sequence with a pause for the other person’s reply. In the app, compare the casual, everyday polite and formal versions to notice how endings change while the main intention remains similar.

A common mistake

Do not use 안녕 with every stranger just because it is shorter. It is casual. Also distinguish the two goodbye phrases by who stays and who leaves, rather than memorizing both as an identical “bye.”

Quick self-check

Answer all three questions, then check your understanding. This is practice, not a proficiency certificate.

1. A useful first greeting to an unfamiliar adult is…

안녕하세요 is a practical polite greeting.

2. You leave and the other person stays. Say…

계세요 addresses the person who is staying.

3. Should you start casual speech with every younger stranger?

Age alone does not establish a relationship for casual speech.
