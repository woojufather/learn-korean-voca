Batchim: Korean Final Consonants for Beginners

받침, often written batchim in English, is a consonant at the bottom of a Korean syllable block. It affects both the shape of the block and the way the word sounds. A final consonant is not always pronounced like the same letter at the beginning of a syllable.

In this lesson

  1. Locate the final consonant
  2. Learn seven basic ending sounds
  3. Close the sound without adding a vowel
  4. Link into a following vowel
  5. Practise words in context
  6. Listen to examples

Locate the final consonant

Compare 가 and 간. The second block has ㄴ underneath the vowel: that is its 받침. In 밥, the final ㅂ closes the syllable. Some blocks have two written final consonants, such as 읽. Reading those clusters requires further rules, so begin with simple final consonants before trying to memorize every cluster.

Learn seven basic ending sounds

At a pause or before many consonants, Korean final sounds fall into seven basic categories: ㄱ, ㄴ, ㄷ, ㄹ, ㅁ, ㅂ and ㅇ. Different written letters can share an ending sound. For example, 옷 ends with a ㄷ-type sound when said alone, even though the written final letter is ㅅ. These categories are a starting point; surrounding sounds can cause additional changes.

Close the sound without adding a vowel

Final ㄱ, ㄷ and ㅂ are normally unreleased in isolation. Close the relevant part of the mouth without adding a strong puff or another syllable. 국 should not gain an extra “ku,” and 밥 should not end in an extra “bu.” Final ㄴ and ㅁ can be held more easily, which makes them useful first practice sounds.

Link into a following vowel

When a suitable final consonant is followed by a vowel-initial ending, it can link into the next syllable. 밥 + 이 is written 밥이 and is pronounced roughly [바비]. 옷 + 이 becomes 옷이, pronounced [오시]. Here brackets show pronunciation, not replacement spelling. Keep the original spelling when writing.

Practise words in context

Listen first to 밥 on its own, then 밥이 있어요. Compare 옷 with 옷이. A full sentence lets you hear why reading every block in isolation is not enough. Rules such as nasalization, tensification and changes involving ㅎ come later. Learn a few frequent phrases accurately and add rules as you encounter them.

A common mistake

Do not replace the written word with a pronunciation spelling. 옷이 is still written 옷이 even though it sounds like [오시]. Also avoid applying the seven ending sounds mechanically before every vowel.

Quick self-check

Answer all three questions, then check your understanding. This is practice, not a proficiency certificate.

1. What is 받침?

받침 is the written final consonant position.

2. How is 밥이 commonly pronounced?

ㅂ links into the vowel-initial particle 이.

3. Should you add an extra vowel after final ㄱ?

Keep the final consonant attached without an extra syllable.
