Ask for Directions in Korean: 어디예요?

Asking for directions is only half the task: you also need a way to understand or clarify the answer. Learn one location question, a few direction words and a polite request to repeat. Keep your destination visible on your phone if its name is unfamiliar.

In this lesson

  1. Ask where a place is
  2. Listen for right, left and straight
  3. Check distance and landmarks
  4. Ask for help when you lose the thread
  5. Build a small dialogue
  6. Listen to examples
  7. Quick self-check

Ask where a place is

화장실이 어디예요? means “Where is the restroom?” 어디 asks where. After a noun ending in a consonant, 이 can mark the subject; after a vowel, 가 is used. 서울역이 어디예요? follows the same pattern. In everyday speech a particle may be omitted, but learning the complete pattern gives you a useful base.

Listen for right, left and straight

오른쪽 means right, 왼쪽 means left and 쭉 means straight or continuously along. 오른쪽으로 가세요 asks you to go to the right. 쭉 가세요 tells you to continue straight. A speaker may point as well. Repeat the key direction back to confirm rather than trying to memorize a long reply in one hearing.

Check distance and landmarks

여기서 멀어요? asks whether it is far from here. 가까워요 means it is close. 출구 is an exit, 입구 is an entrance and 길 is a road or way. In a station, a numbered exit may be more useful than a street address. If staff show you a sign, match the Korean place name carefully to the one you need.

Ask for help when you lose the thread

천천히 말해 주세요 means “Please speak slowly.” 다시 한 번 말해 주세요 asks for one more repetition. 지도에서 보여 주세요 asks someone to show you on the map. These are normal communication tools, not signs of failure. A short clarification can prevent a much longer detour.

Build a small dialogue

You: 화장실이 어디예요? Other person: 쭉 가서 오른쪽으로 가세요. You: 오른쪽이요? 감사합니다. The second line means to go straight and then turn right. In practice, directions depend on the building and street. Learn the language pattern rather than memorizing the sample as navigation advice for a real location.

A common mistake

Do not confuse 오른쪽 and 왼쪽 just because you recognize the sentence ending. Confirm the direction word. These examples teach language; check a current map for your actual route.

Quick self-check

Answer all three questions, then check your understanding. This is practice, not a proficiency certificate.

1. Which word means where?

어디 asks where; 얼마 asks how much and 언제 asks when.

2. What does 왼쪽 mean?

왼쪽 is left.

3. Which asks for a map explanation?

지도에서 보여 주세요 asks to be shown on a map.
