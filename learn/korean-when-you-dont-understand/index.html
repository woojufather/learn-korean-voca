What to Say When You Do Not Understand Korean

You do not need to understand every word to keep a conversation going. A small set of repair phrases lets you ask for a slower pace, another explanation or a written word. Learn these early so that you can use real conversations as practice.

In this lesson

  1. Say what you need clearly
  2. Ask for one more repetition
  3. Ask about meaning or writing
  4. Mention your current level
  5. Turn a repair into learning
  6. Listen to examples
  7. Quick self-check

Say what you need clearly

잘 모르겠어요 means that you are not sure or do not really understand. Pair it with a specific request: 천천히 말해 주세요 asks the speaker to slow down. This gives the other person something helpful to do. Simply smiling and saying 네 may hide a misunderstanding until it becomes harder to fix.

Ask for one more repetition

다시 한 번 말해 주세요 means “Please say that once more.” Listen for the part you missed rather than trying to translate everything at once. If a particular word is the problem, repeat that word with a questioning tone or point to it. A shorter clarification is often easier for both speakers.

Ask about meaning or writing

이게 무슨 뜻이에요? means “What does this mean?” 여기 써 주세요 asks someone to write it here. Written Korean can help with names, menu items or addresses. Do not show personal information to strangers unnecessarily; a blank note on your phone is enough for a language example.

Mention your current level

한국어를 배우고 있어요 means “I am learning Korean.” It explains why you may need a little more time. 영어 할 수 있어요? asks whether someone can speak English, but do not assume the answer will be yes. You can combine a Korean repair phrase, a gesture and a written word to communicate even without a shared fluent language.

Turn a repair into learning

After you understand, repeat the useful word and thank the speaker. Later, save a short phrase in your own notes and practise it again. For example: 잘 모르겠어요. 다시 한 번 말해 주세요. Then: 아, 알겠어요. 감사합니다. The goal is not a flawless performance; it is checking meaning and continuing respectfully.

A common mistake

Do not automatically answer 네 when you have not understood an important detail. Ask a specific question, especially about a price, destination or arrangement. Repeating a memorized sentence is most useful when it solves a real communication problem.

Quick self-check

Answer all three questions, then check your understanding. This is practice, not a proficiency certificate.

1. Which asks someone to speak slowly?

천천히 means slowly.

2. What does 다시 한 번 request?

다시 한 번 means once again.

3. What asks about a word’s meaning?

무슨 뜻 asks about meaning.
