Korean Restaurant Phrases: Ordering and Paying

At a Korean restaurant, a few clear phrases can carry you from finding a table to paying. Start with polite requests and listen for the staff’s reply. Restaurant routines vary, so use the language to clarify rather than assuming every place works the same way.

In this lesson

  1. Tell the staff how many people
  2. Point, name and request
  3. Ask about food you may not know
  4. Handle small requests politely
  5. Pay and close the exchange
  6. Listen to examples
  7. Quick self-check

Tell the staff how many people

두 명이에요 means “There are two of us.” 명 counts people in ordinary situations. For one person, say 한 명이에요. Staff may guide you to a table or indicate a self-service area. If you are not sure where to sit, 여기 앉아도 돼요? politely asks whether you may sit here.

Point, name and request

이거 하나 주세요 means “One of this, please.” It works while pointing at a menu item. If you know the name, 비빔밥 하나 주세요 is more specific. Some menus have minimum portions or items for sharing; the phrase itself does not override those conditions. Watch and listen for a clarification about servings.

Ask about food you may not know

이거 매워요? asks whether a dish is spicy. 덜 맵게 해 주세요 asks for a less spicy version, when that is possible. If you have an allergy, do not rely on a generic preference such as “not spicy.” Name the ingredient, explain the allergy and confirm how the dish is prepared with staff.

Handle small requests politely

물 좀 주세요 asks for water. 여기요 can politely attract a server’s attention, but use a normal voice and wait. Water, side dishes or utensils may be self-service in some places. If you do not understand a reply, ask 다시 한 번 말해 주세요 rather than pretending you understood.

Pay and close the exchange

계산해 주세요 asks to pay. 카드 돼요? asks whether a card can be used. In many restaurants you pay at a counter near the exit, though the process varies. After a meal, 잘 먹었습니다 is a courteous way to say you enjoyed or appreciated the meal. Practise these phrases as a sequence with pauses for answers.

A common mistake

Do not treat 덜 맵게 as an allergy warning. It only asks for less spiciness. For ordinary requests, remember 주세요 instead of using an abrupt item name on its own.

Quick self-check

Answer all three questions, then check your understanding. This is practice, not a proficiency certificate.

1. Which tells a restaurant there are two people?

명 is a common counter for people.

2. Which asks whether a dish is spicy?

매워요? asks about spiciness.

3. When you want to pay, say…

계산해 주세요 is a useful request to pay.
