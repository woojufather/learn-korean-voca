Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current operation of K-Talk Coach at ktalkcoach.online. The operator is zyper choi, reachable at wooju200903@gmail.com.

Learning data on your device

The app uses browser local storage for settings, selected language, learning progress, review items and attendance. These are not an online account or a cloud backup. You can remove them by clearing this site’s browser storage. Offline app files can also be stored by a service worker.

Speech and microphone features

Listening uses the browser’s speech synthesis service. Depending on your installed voice and browser, speech may be processed on your device or by its speech provider. Microphone practice runs only when you choose it and grant permission. Browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser provider. K-Talk Coach does not record or store audio in its own database. Check your browser provider’s privacy information before using voice features.

Feedback and contact

Submitting a form sends your message, category, language and optional reply email to a Cloudflare Worker and D1 database. In the app, you can choose whether to attach the screen, Korean example and broad device/browser information shown in the preview. The Contact page does not attach that context. Messages are visible through the authenticated administrator inbox and are not public. Your email is used to follow up on your request.

Direct emails are handled through the operator’s email provider. Feedback is retained for handling requests and maintaining the service; there is currently no automatic scheduled deletion. To request removal, contact the operator with your message reference or enough detail to identify it. Avoid submitting sensitive information.

First-party learning statistics

The site sends limited usage events to its own Cloudflare service: page views, learning starts, answered questions, quiz completions, started microphone practice and selected interface language. Events include a fixed page identifier, language and broad device category, not your answers, query strings, full browsing history or microphone audio. A random browser identifier is saved locally and converted to a keyed hash before storage in D1. Server receipt time is used for daily statistics in Korea time (UTC+09:00).

Visitors are estimated by browser, not verified people. Administrator browsers and recognized bots are excluded. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected. Offline, blocked or storage-disabled activity can be missing. Existing records do not have an automatic scheduled deletion; the operator can exclude identifiable browser records from aggregate reports. Clearing local storage may prevent the operator from linking a past browser identifier to a later request.

This choice concerns K-Talk Coach’s own statistics. It does not control Google advertising or a browser provider’s speech service.

Advertising and Google services

The homepage includes Google AdSense verification and advertising library code for publisher ca-pub-5677208926480734. Approval and ad serving are controlled by Google. If ads are served, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to deliver, personalize and measure ads. See how Google uses data from partner sites, Google advertising technologies and My Ad Center.

Google Analytics 4 is not currently configured by this release. First-party server statistics are separate from GA4. If GA4 is connected later, this policy and the consent setup must be updated before it is enabled. Google advertising consent, where required, is managed through an appropriate consent platform; the first-party statistics buttons above are not a Google-certified advertising consent platform.

Hosting, security and retention

GitHub Pages serves the website. Cloudflare processes form requests and first-party statistics. CDN providers deliver some app libraries. These providers receive network information needed to handle a request and may process it under their own policies. Our application database does not save raw IP addresses or full user-agent strings for analytics. Abuse limits use a daily keyed hash derived from the IP address; expired buckets are cleaned during form handling. Infrastructure providers may separately keep their own operational logs.

Administrator sessions expire after four hours and are stored as hashes on the server. Administrative access requires authentication. No system can guarantee absolute security. The site does not sell feedback email addresses or publish individual feedback.

Your choices and requests

Microphone use and feedback are optional. You can disable microphone permission in your browser, clear local progress, turn off first-party statistics above or contact the operator about information you submitted. We may need enough information to locate a record without disclosing someone else’s data. The site is intended for general learners; do not submit children’s personal information. Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date.
