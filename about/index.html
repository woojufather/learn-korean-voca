About the project

Practical Korean you can use.

K-Talk Coach helps beginners connect Korean letters and words with everyday conversations. The site is operated by zyper choi.

What you can learn

The app includes a Hangul syllable builder, vocabulary quizzes, flashcards, review tools and 60 everyday conversation situations. Speech-level comparisons help learners notice the difference between casual, everyday polite and formal language.

The beginner lesson library explains common questions in English. The practice app offers interface and learning support in English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish and Thai.

How practice works

Read an explanation, listen to an example and try recalling it before checking the answer. Your personal progress is stored in your browser. An account is not required for ordinary learning. Clearing your browser data can remove your saved progress.

Audio uses your device’s Korean speech voices. Microphone practice is optional and depends on browser support. Recognized text is a learning aid, not a professional pronunciation or intonation assessment.

Accuracy and corrections

Korean expressions vary by situation, relationship and region. Examples are intended as beginner practice. If an explanation, translation or audio behaves unexpectedly, please send a correction. Do not rely on a learning example for medical, emergency or other critical communication.
